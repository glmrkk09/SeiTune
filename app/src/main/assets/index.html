Third-party tools

The web interface uses no outside libraries, frameworks or CDNs. The app is a native Android WebView. Searching and downloading are done by yt-dlp (Unlicense), which is bundled inside the app (python and yt-dlp, updatable from this screen). Streams, downloads and the offline cache are handled by yt-dlp. Thumbnails load from i.ytimg.com. The look and feel is shared with CubeLiteX, also by Glmrk.

Your music

Imported music folders are only read, never changed or copied: the app keeps your permission to read that folder and a list of its songs. Remove a folder from Library > Imported at any time. Downloads and the cache are stored on your device (Android/data/com.mrk.seitune/files) and nothing is sent to anyone except the sites you search and download from. Only download content you have the right to download.

Video quality

Streams are the media file itself, so there are no ads. Streamed video is limited to the single-file formats YouTube offers (usually 360p); cached and downloaded video are merged at the quality above.

Save folder

Extra downloads wait in a queue and start by themselves. Audio as M4A is the fastest (no conversion); MP3 is converted on the phone and takes longer.
